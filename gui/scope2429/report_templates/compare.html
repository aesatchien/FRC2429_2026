    How the numbers are made

    • Source: @@NLOGS@@ robot .wpilog files with at least 60 s enabled (@@NOLD@@ from prior comps, @@NNEW@@ from @@NEWNAME@@). Empty logs and short replay fragments are excluded.
    • Current and voltage are the PDH total current and battery voltage the robot code publishes, time-weighted over the enabled windows. Sampling is 5 to 18 Hz, which differs by event, so peaks are not exact.
    • Camera down, link drops and reboots come from each camera's frame counter and the NetworkTables connection log. Kraken data comes from the converted CTRE hoot files, aligned on the robot-enable edges.
    • Prior comps used a different robot code base and the roboRIO; the robot hardware may also differ. Differences smaller than the spread between matches within one event are not conclusions.
    • "Drops within 3 s after a spike" is compared with the share of enabled time that falls within 3 s after a spike, which is what a random drop would show.
